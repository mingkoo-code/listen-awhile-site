For press outside Korea

Listen Awhile is a sound app that never touches the microphone. Once a day it delivers a single listening prompt, then blocks all input for 30 seconds — the remaining time appears only as one thin ring, never as a number. When the 30 seconds end, you write one line about what you heard.

Nothing is recorded. There is no account, no server, no ads. Entries stay on the device. iPhone, free, in Korean and English.

The screenshots and icon above are free to use. For anything else, write to listenawhile.app@gmail.com.
